UNDER THE SCREENS

RAW LAB
METHODS.

We do not press buttons on automated carousels. Every single piece is loaded, pulled, and cured by hand under high tension.

The T-Shirt Lab was founded in an unheated industrial warehouse with one simple goal: to resurrect the heavy, high-contrast print feel that automated digital garment printers killed. We formulate custom ink systems that bite deep into heavyweight combed cotton fibers, creating merchandise that survives years of heavy wear.

01 WATER SOLUTIONS

Water-Based Discharge Ink

Instead of laying plastic-feeling ink on top of fibers, our discharge system strips the dye out of the shirt and replaces it with color, leaving a completely weightless feel after the first wash.

REDUCED CARBON FOOTPRINT →
02 HIGH-VOLUME STRENGTH

Phthalate-Free Plastisol

When heavy opacity is required, we use next-generation, non-toxic plastisol ink formulas. Perfect for blocky, high-visibility street graphics that won't crack under heat.

100% TOXIN ACCREDITED →
03 ZERO DRAIN WASTE

Closed-Loop Reclaim

We operate a closed-loop screen cleaning filtration station. Every drop of ink degrader is recycled and filtered to prevent hazardous solids from ever entering the community water systems.
