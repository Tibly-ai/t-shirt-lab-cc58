BUILT TO OUTLAST THE REST

01/

RAW WEIGHT COTTON

We exclusively stock and print on premium blanks from 6.5 oz up to 10 oz combed open-end cotton. Rigid fit, wide shoulders, durable construction.

02/

PLASTISOL SYSTEM

High-viscosity ink cured under strict high temperatures. The print sits thick on the fabric, maintaining vivid opacity even after countless wash cycles.

TENSION CONTROL

Screens are stretched to precise newton levels before loading onto our industrial press. Every dot, alignment, and hairline remains dead-accurate.
